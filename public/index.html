Official UNO Rules

Objective

Be the first player to play all cards from your hand. You score points from the cards left in opponents' hands. First to 250 points wins the match!

Matching Rules

On your turn, you must match a card from your hand to the card on top of the Discard pile, either by Color, Number, or Action Symbol.

Action Cards

Draw Two (+2): Next player must draw 2 cards and lose their turn.

Reverse (⇄): Reverses direction of play. (In a 2-player game, acts as a Skip!)

Skip (⊘): Next player is skipped.

Wild: Can be played on any turn. You declare the next active color.

Wild Draw Four (+4): Next player draws 4 cards and loses their turn. You declare the color. Can be challenged under official rules!

Calling UNO

When you have only 1 card left, you MUST hit the "UNO!" button. If you forget and another player presses "CATCH!", you must draw 2 penalty cards!

House Rules (Configurable)

Stacking: Stack +2 on +2 or +4 on +4 to pass the draw penalty to the next player!

7-0 Rule: Playing a 7 lets you swap hands with anyone. Playing a 0 rotates all hands in turn direction!

Jump-In: Play an exact identical card immediately even out of turn!
